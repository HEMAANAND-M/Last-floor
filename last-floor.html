The Last Floor

      
You wake up in a building that shouldn't exist. Every floor is almost the same... except for one thing.

      
Nine chapters, ninety-four floors. Each one plays differently. Chapter 3 has jumpscares, which you can turn off in Settings.

What to expect

      
A hallway that changes when you look away. A robot who is too helpful. A floor with no lights and something that only moves when your back is turned.

      
Abilities unlock as you climb, and the Floors menu lets you replay any floor you've reached. Forty-two memory fragments and one secret are hidden across the chapters.
